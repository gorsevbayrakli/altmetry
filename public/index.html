How the AI transition works

  1. 01

    Listen

    We talk to the team one by one and look at the processes, the data and the tools. The spreadsheet fixed by hand every Monday is usually where it starts.

  2. 02

    Prioritise

    We pick the two or three chances with the biggest effect and write down how each one will be measured, before anything is built.

  3. 03

    Build a pilot

    One process, your existing tools, inside your team. You don't have to move to a new system.

  4. 04

    Put it to work

    The pilot runs on real work. We watch where it stumbles, fix it, train the team and write the rules.

  5. 05

    Grow what works

    We compare the numbers with the starting point and carry what worked to other teams and processes.

What we've written

Short pieces on consulting and the move to AI, from what we have seen in real teams.

  1. Rules and measurement

    “We use AI” and “we get value from AI” are two different sentences

    How many people use it says little on its own. Measure adoption and value apart, and three simple measures are enough for a small company: time spent on the task, how often it comes back with errors, and how many days a week the team uses the tool.

  2. Sales and marketing

    Your most valuable proposal data is your past proposals

    Most of a proposal was already written in earlier ones. The time goes into finding the right old file and fixing what is out of date. An assistant fed by your library writes the first draft; price, special terms and the final check stay with a person.

  3. Rules and measurement

    An AI use policy for a 10-person company fits on one page

    Four questions: which tools are approved, which data never goes into any tool, what an AI agent may touch and who approves it, and who to tell when something goes wrong. Read it once with the team, update it every three months.

  4. Sales and marketing

    What warms a cold message is knowing the other side's agenda

    Lowering the price rarely wins a company that has said no for years. Opening with something useful about their market does. That research used to be done by hand for each firm; AI can now do it for hundreds, and every message is still written for one reader.

  5. Process improvement

    A pilot is there to show where it breaks

    Most AI projects start with a big plan and stay in the slide deck. We go the other way: one process, the success measure written first, four to six weeks on real work, and we grow it only if the numbers hold.
